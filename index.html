Background

About Me

I am a B.Tech Information Technology student learning web development through practical, hands-on projects.

My journey in technology centers on understanding how modern web interfaces are designed and structured from the ground up. Rather than relying solely on abstract theory, I focus on building functional websites, testing layouts across various devices, and applying clean coding principles in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

What I am actively interested in:

  • Responsive Web Design: Creating adaptable layouts that provide an intuitive viewing experience across mobile phones, tablets, and widescreen desktops.
  • Frontend Development: Writing semantic HTML5, well-structured CSS3 with modern flexbox and grid, and clean vanilla JavaScript for interactive components.
  • Building Websites for Small Businesses: Crafting clean, accessible digital presence for local stores, dining establishments, and service businesses to help them connect with customers.
  • Learning New Development Tools & Technologies: Continuously exploring modern developer tooling, version control workflows, browser performance practices, and web accessibility standards.
